# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

zcoder contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate professionally, review ideas and code on technical merit, respect privacy and security boundaries, and make collaboration welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Acceptable participation includes constructive technical feedback, clear evidence for claims, respectful disagreement, responsible security disclosure, and good-faith efforts to improve reliability, safety, accessibility, and maintainability.

Unacceptable participation includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets or private data, public exploitation of unpatched vulnerabilities, spam, or repeated disruption of project collaboration.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project documentation, release coordination, and other spaces where someone is representing the zcoder project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, remove, lock, or reject contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project. Security-sensitive material may be moved to private reporting channels.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer through the contact information associated with the project. For vulnerabilities or accidental secret exposure, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of opening a public issue.
